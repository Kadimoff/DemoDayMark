How it works

An event in four steps.

  1. 01

    Sign up

    You create the event and each team gets its own link and QR code. Everyone on the team signs in with the same link.

  2. 02
    PDFPPTXURL

    Upload

    Teams upload a PDF or PPTX deck and their project site link. Your dashboard shows who has uploaded and who hasn't yet.

  3. 03

    Stage

    The deck opens on the big screen with the timer in the corner. You run the timer from your phone: start, pause, next team.

  4. 04

    Results

    Judges score each criterion on their phones. When the last pitch ends, the leaderboard is already done.

FAQ

Common questions

How do people sign in?

Coordinators use “Sign in”. Judges scan the QR code the coordinator shows them, and teams open the link the coordinator sends. It all happens on this site.

What do teams upload?

A PDF or PPTX deck (up to 50 MB) and their project site link. They can also add a demo video link and a logo. Files can be replaced until the deadline.

What do I need for the stage screen?

Any computer with a browser plus a projector or LED wall. Open the stage link in the browser and go full screen; there is nothing to install.

How do I control the timer?

From the remote on your phone: start, pause, add time, move to the next team. Every connected screen updates at the same moment.

How do judges score?

They scan the QR code the coordinator shows them, type their name and start scoring. Each QR code works once. Averages and the ranking are calculated for you.

What language is the app in?

The app interface is in Azerbaijani. Team names, projects and criteria can be written in any language.
