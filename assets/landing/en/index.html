How it works

From sign-up to verdict — four steps, one link.

  1. 01

    Sign up

    The organiser creates the event, teams join with a single link. No spreadsheets, email chains or printed lists.

  2. 02
    PDFPPTXURL

    Upload

    Teams upload their deck and their project site link. Every upload lands instantly as a notification for the organiser.

  3. 03

    Stage

    The deck is on the big screen, the timer floats above it. The remote lives on the organiser’s phone: start, pause, next team.

  4. 04

    Verdict

    Judges score each criterion from their own device. The leaderboard updates live — results are ready when the last pitch ends.

FAQ

Frequently asked

How do people sign in?

Organisers use “Sign in”, jurors scan the QR code the organiser shows them, and teams open the team link the organiser sends — all on this site.

What can participants upload?

A presentation file and their project site link. Each team sees its materials on its own card and can update them until the deadline.

What do I need for the stage screen?

Any computer with a browser plus a projector or LED wall. Open the stage link in full screen — nothing to install.

How do I control the timer?

From your phone: start, pause, add time, move to the next team. Every connected screen updates at the same moment.

How do judges score?

From their own phones or tablets, by criteria the organiser defines. Averages and the leaderboard are calculated automatically.

Which languages are supported?

Azerbaijani and English.
